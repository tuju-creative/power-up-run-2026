FAQ

Is registration free?

Yes. The 5KM Fun Run is free of charge (FOC). You register on Luma.

How many runners can join?

Up to 1,000 participants can take part in the 5KM Fun Run.

When does registration open and close?

Registration opens on 2 November 2026 and closes on 16 November 2026.

How do I get my confirmation?

After you register on Luma you receive an email confirmation with a QR code. Show the QR code on race day.

What is in the runner's kit?

The runner's kit includes an event T-shirt, a finisher medal, a tote bag and a timing chip.

Is there parking available?

Parking details will be shared by email before race day.

Can I change my category?

There is only one category, the 5KM Fun Run, so there is nothing to change.
